Market Power Brief

The power picture for one U.S. data-center market: what's running, what's queued, how long new loads wait, and how it stacks up against the markets you compete with.

Written by Eugene Barber · built from public, cited sources · delivered as a PDF in 7 business days

Every data-center conversation now runs into the same question: can the grid actually serve it, and when? The public answer is scattered across utility filings, grid-operator queue reports, county agendas and broker market reports. A Market Power Brief pulls it together for the one market you care about, with every figure traced to its source.

It's built for economic development teams preparing for prospects or board decisions, site selectors shortlisting markets, and developers and investors sizing up a new region.

Frequently asked

Which markets can you cover?

Any of the 16 markets on the regions page, and most other U.S. metros or counties with active data-center development. If public data is too thin, you'll be told before work starts and refunded.

Is this an interconnection or engineering study?

No. It's desk research from public sources: EIA, LBNL, grid-operator and utility filings, CBRE and public records. It isn't a load-flow or interconnection study, an engineering assessment or legal advice, and it can't promise power at a specific site.
